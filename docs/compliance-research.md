# Compliance research register

**An issue register, not legal advice or clearance.** It records which rules may apply to a *future real-money* version of StableFlow, where to read them, and what remains unknown. The testnet prototype moves no real value. That does not establish an exemption or production readiness (plan §13). Any real-money design needs qualified Brazilian and US counsel.

- Corridor assumed: payer in the US, freelancer (individual) resident in Brazil, payout in BRL.
- Business-activity assumption: a platform that issues payment requests, receives USDC on behalf of or directly to a freelancer, converts to BRL and pays out to a Brazilian bank account. Whether it would custody funds is **undecided**, and that choice changes most rows below.
- All items were checked on **2026-10-29** by Claude Code (web search plus official pages where reachable). **Source quality** says whether a claim was read on the official page or taken from a secondary summary. Secondary claims must be verified before anyone relies on them.
- Owner for every follow-up: Julio, until a product owner or counsel is assigned.

## Conclusion for product scope

The biggest finding is **C3**: since February 2026, Brazil treats this flow's core action (stablecoin → BRL for an international payment) as an FX operation for authorized institutions. A real StableFlow would almost certainly operate through a licensed FX or SPSAV partner rather than as an unlicensed intermediary. The product problem then becomes partner integration, KYC handoff and disclosure, not settlement technology.
